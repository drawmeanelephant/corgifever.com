# About

Corgi Fever is a tiny boutique site about a fictional ailment. It is kept deliberately small.

## What it is

- Three pages about noticing corgis a little too much.
- A bit of deadpan lore, written for amusement.

## What it is not

- Not a registry. No dogs are catalogued here.
- Not an authority on breeds, bloodlines, health, or training.
- Not medical advice. Corgi Fever is not real, and this site proves nothing.
