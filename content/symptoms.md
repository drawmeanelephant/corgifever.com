# Symptoms

Sufferers tend to report the same things, in roughly the same order.

## The early signs

- Stopping for every low, long dog on the street, regardless of schedule.
- Referring to ordinary legs as "short" and ordinary walks as "waddles."
- Saying *sploot* in conversation and expecting to be understood.

## The settled condition

- Knowing where the neighborhood corgis live, by name, without having been told.
- Crouching to dog height for photographs as a matter of principle.
- Feeling briefly, unreasonably cheered by the sight of a loaf-shaped animal.

## A note

This is lore, not diagnosis. Corgi Fever is fictional. If a real dog needs care, ask a real veterinarian — this site knows nothing about joints, coats, or diets, and makes no claims about them.
